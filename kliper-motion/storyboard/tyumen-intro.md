# Tyumen Intro

Duration: 12 seconds  
Format: 1920x1080, 30 fps  
Composition: `TyumenIntro`

## Timeline

| Time | Visual |
| --- | --- |
| 0-2s | Clouds above Tyumen, city barely visible. |
| 2-4s | Descent toward the city and river. |
| 4-6s | Bird/airplane-height movement over residential districts. |
| 6-8s | District/property nodes glow over the city. |
| 8-10s | Nodes become abstract property cards. |
| 10-12s | Cards converge into a website interface portal. |

## Image-To-Video Prompts

### 01 to 02

Smooth cinematic camera push-in through soft morning clouds toward Tyumen city. The cloud layer parts naturally, revealing the river and city grid. Stable airplane-to-drone movement, realistic parallax, premium real estate film look. No shaking, no warping, no text, no map controls.

### 02 to 03

Smooth forward flight from broad city view toward residential districts. Maintain realistic scale, stable horizon, gradual altitude decrease, road and building parallax, soft morning haze. Avoid fast zoom, building deformation, flicker, and extra landmarks.

### 03 to 04

Camera continues gliding over the district as subtle luminous real-estate nodes and soft area highlights appear on the ground perspective. Overlay must feel integrated, minimal, and premium. No readable labels, no cluttered HUD.

### 04 to 05

Glowing property nodes rise from the city and transform into abstract floating real-estate cards. Camera pushes toward the forming interface grid. Keep city below softly visible. No fake letters, no logos, no chaotic UI.

### 05 to 06

Camera flies into the central luminous interface panel. Floating cards converge into a clean website surface, preparing for a seamless cut to the real Kliper.City site. Smooth centered motion, no browser chrome, no fake text, no watermark.
